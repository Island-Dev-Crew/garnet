# Garnet Promo Visual Design

## Style Prompt

Build the 30-second Garnet promo as a precise evidence-driven product film: dark graphite canvas, sharp ruby-garnet accent, white-hot proof text, and restrained motion that feels like an engineering workbench becoming presentation-ready. The visual language should show real Garnet surfaces and readiness evidence, not abstract marketing decoration.

## Colors

- `#050608`: graphite canvas and negative space.
- `#f04f66`: garnet accent for logo glow, active gates, and timeline markers.
- `#f8fafc`: primary text and proof labels.
- `#23d3ee`: PWA/workbench signal and secondary proof highlights.
- `#f6c95f`: caution color for remaining gated work.

## Typography

- Use system UI typography: `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif.
- Use tabular numeric treatment for timing, gate counts, and readiness metrics.

## Motion Rules

- Favor short, directional reveals: vertical lift, subtle scale, and opacity.
- Keep all motion deterministic and finite.
- Animate from the static hero layout into place; do not position key content by animation alone.
- Make the five storyboard beats legible at 1920x1080 without requiring narration.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use generic blue/purple gradients as the main identity.
- Do not imply a rendered promo video, notarized app, mobile app, or provider-backed converter exists.
- Do not use abstract blobs, floating orbs, or stock-like atmospheric imagery.
- Do not hide current readiness caveats behind decorative motion.
