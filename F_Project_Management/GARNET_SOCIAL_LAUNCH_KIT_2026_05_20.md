## Launch Copy

### X / Bluesky Short

Garnet v0.5.0 is live.

Rust rigor. Ruby velocity. Agent-native memory, explicit `@caps(...)`, signed
hot-reload demos, deterministic build manifests, LSP source, fuzzing, and
honest productization status.

Research-grade prototype, not production-complete.

https://garnet-lang.org/blog/posts/2026-05-20-why-garnet-exists.html

### LinkedIn

Today I published Garnet v0.5.0, a research-grade dual-mode language platform:
managed-mode ergonomics for orchestration, safe-mode rigor for hot paths, and
agent-native primitives for memory, capabilities, signed hot-reload, and
deterministic build manifests.

The important part is not that every product gate is closed. It is that the
project now says precisely what is real: six v0.5 blocking slices are merged,
the MIT/productization pulse is higher and more granular, the public site links
live Discussions, and the remaining gates are visible instead of hidden.

First post: https://garnet-lang.org/blog/posts/2026-05-20-why-garnet-exists.html
