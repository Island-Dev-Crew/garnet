# Garnet 12-domain / 7-novel proof matrix (S48)

Rigor evidence for a skeptical reviewer (reconciliation §44, §150): a
falsifiable inventory of the 12 application domains Garnet is demonstrated across
and the 7 novel Paper VI contributions, each anchored to in-repo evidence whose
existence is checked. The live tables are regenerated by
`scripts/garnet_proof_matrix.py --format md`; `--gate` runs in CI and fails if a
domain example or a contribution anchor disappears.

## Honest scope — evidence inventory, not proof (Paper VI anchors preserved)

This matrix is an **evidence inventory**, not empirical proof and **not** a
re-adjudication of Paper VI's per-contribution verdicts. It makes **no**
measurement, mechanized-proof, or external-study claim. Two contribution-
numbering schemes exist across the repo's docs, so the seven contributions are
listed **by title** and Paper VI's own aggregate scorecard is quoted **verbatim**
rather than assigning a support verdict per contribution:

> **Paper VI scorecard:** "4 supported, 2 partial (downgraded honestly),
> 0 refuted, 1 pending-infra"

What the matrix proves is narrower and falsifiable: every contribution is
*exercised* by in-repo evidence that exists, and all 12 domain examples are
present (and are the `CORE_12_CASES` of the studio domain-matrix suite).
